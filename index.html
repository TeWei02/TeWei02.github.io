I'm Te-Wei Ko, a freshman at STUST's Computer Science department with an unconventional background spanning semiconductor engineering and philosophy. My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, and cognitive science — driven by a conviction that technology should adapt to human cognition, not the other way around.

My core project, ModernReader, applies a Cognitive Rhythm Alignment (CRA) model to dynamically tune reading interfaces to the user's real-time cognitive state. I also build tools like Sensory Shield to reduce digital noise for neurodiverse users, and Project H.O.L.O., an AI framework that reimagines storytelling beyond the constraints of text.

Projects

Core Project

ModernReader

AI-powered reading assistance platform built on the Cognitive Rhythm Alignment (CRA) model. Tracks scrolling behavior, reading speed, and attention metrics in real-time to dynamically adapt content presentation — reducing cognitive load and improving comprehension.
